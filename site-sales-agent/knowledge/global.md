# SKY ALLEYS — GLOBAL CONTEXT

## Company Overview
Sky Alleys operates a dual-engine commercial model combining Business Development 
as a Service (BDaaS) with Expert-Led Delivery, underpinned by a Board & Executive 
Advisory Practice that generates decision-grade evidence for enterprise technology 
spend. Sky Alleys does not sell effort or strategy decks — it delivers evidence-based, 
outcome-funded engagements designed to eliminate structural misalignments in 
traditional enterprise IT consulting.

## Expert Network (80/10/10)
- 80% → Member Commission (paid strictly upon client receipt)
- 10% → Network Growth Fund (marketing, events, expansion)
- 10% → Resilience Pool (redistributed to active members)
- Senior practitioners only → contribution-based
- No pyramid | No leverage | No scarcity
- Active Member Eligibility: Good standing (signed charter) AND active contribution
- Anti-Free-Riding Controls: Observer status for inactive; quarterly reviews

## Competitive Positioning (Big 4 vs Boutiques vs Sky Alleys)
- Big 4: High fixed overheads, billable-hour models, 8-16 week cycles, junior-heavy
- Boutiques: Lower rates, 4-8 weeks, variable senior oversight
- Sky Alleys: Outcome-funded, senior-only delivery (20+ years), 10-day Decision 
  Dossiers, 3-week Board Health Checks, agent go-lives in 2-4 weeks

## Objection Handling
- "Too small": Senior operators who ran $5M+ budgets buy-side. Board-grade gate in 
  10 days at a fraction of Big 4 commitment.
- "Is this scalable?": Governed frameworks and CWA built on existing M365/Azure. 
  Enterprise scale without headcount expansion.
- "Single-person dependency?": 80/10/10 expert network under auditable Network 
  Charter. Governance embedded in infrastructure, not locked in a consultant's head.
- "Already have an MSP": We don't replace MSPs. Vendor Power Reset maps contract 
  vs actual delivery. 80% change request suppression, 30% commercial recovery.
- "AI moving too fast, should wait": Waiting accumulates shadow AI and cost exposure. 
  2-4 week deployments include budget circuit breakers from day one.

## Deal Disqualification (Walk-Away Signals)
- No executive sponsor with budget authority
- "Evaluation only" with no decision timebox within 60 days
- Refusal to share baseline metrics
- Unrealistic custom dev demands where M365 config solves the problem
- No decision-maker access during discovery
