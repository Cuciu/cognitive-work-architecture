# ADVISORY PRACTICE — Board & Executive Advisory

## 1. Decision Dossier — Pre-Board Gate
- Format: 10 business days | Single senior operator | 1 executive deliverable
- Problem: Major tech initiative heading to board; risks and missing costs unvoiced
- Buyers: CFOs, Investment Committees, CIOs presenting business cases, Corporate Strategy
- Domains: Cloud consolidation, Data Center exits, SAP/ERP modernization, Security 
  platforms, AI platforms & data foundations, major vendor renewals
- Deliverables:
  - Decision Dossier (6-8 pages): Single executive narrative, one board recommendation
  - Risk Register (Board Version): Top unvarnished risks, mitigations, ownership
  - Architecture & Operating Model Implications: Second-order impacts
  - Commercial Sanity Check: Missing costs, unsafe assumptions, vendor conditions
- Timeline: Days 1-3 (Review & Discovery) → Days 4-7 (Triangulate & Challenge) → 
  Days 8-10 (Deliver Dossier & Executive Briefing)
- Benchmarks: Up to 30% reduction in cloud run-rate within 6 months; 20% TCO reduction

## 2. Cloud & AI Program Exposure — Board Reality Check
- Format: 3 weeks | 3 execution phases | 1 independent audit-grade view
- Problem: Escalating cloud spend and uncoordinated AI pilots; board lacks clarity 
  on whether spend generates value or funds vendor momentum
- Buyers: Boards of Directors, Audit & Risk Committees, CIOs, CFOs
- Deliverables:
  - Board Brief (10-12 slides): Plain-language for non-technical board members
  - Exposure Map: Top 10 failure modes across cost, security, delivery, compliance
  - Decision Tree: Continue / Stop / Rescope framework with enforceable prerequisites
  - 90-Day Stabilization Plan: Actionable roadmap to restore governance
- Timeline: Week 1 (Stakeholder Interviews) → Week 2 (Triangulation & Gap Analysis) → 
  Week 3 (Board-Ready Synthesis & Q&A)

## 3. Vendor Power Reset — Contract-to-Delivery Forensics
- Format: 2-4 weeks | Forensic-grade evidence gathering
- Problem: Vendor owns delivery narrative; constant change requests and cost escalation
- Buyers: CIOs, Heads of Infrastructure/Cloud, Procurement Leaders
- Deliverables:
  - Contract-to-Delivery Traceability Map: Line-by-line contract vs actual vs invoicing
  - Ambiguity Kill List: Specific clauses causing financial leakage
  - Governance Reset Pack: RACI, decision rights, change control, acceptance criteria
  - Negotiation Brief: Customer leverage points prior to renewal
- Impact Targets: 30% Commercial Recovery; 80% Change Request Suppression; 
  60% Delivery Risk Mitigation; 90% Timeline Alignment

## 4. Executive & Technical Coaching
- Format: Ongoing | Embedded in active programs | Outcome-funded
- Tracks: Enterprise Sales Coaching, BD Coaching, Leader Uplift in Real Programs
- Models: Outcome-Funded Advisory, Co-Invested Initiatives, Executive Stewardship
- 90-Day Onboarding: Weeks 1-2 (Alignment) → Weeks 2-8 (In-Flight Coaching) → 
  Weeks 4-10 (Governance Uplift) → Weeks 10-12 (Value Review & Next Wave)

## Proof Points (Advisory Only)

- Infrastructure: Saved $2M annually for 2 consecutive years
- Data Center Exit: $1.2M annual savings, exceeded business case by 20%
