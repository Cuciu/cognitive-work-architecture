Pillar 1: Board & Executive Advisory Practice (Decision-Grade Gates)

1. Decision Dossier — Pre-Board Gate
Format & Timebox: 10 business days | Single senior operator |
1 executive deliverable package.

Problem Solved: Major technology initiative heading to the board;
business case polished, vendor optimistic, but key risks and missing
costs remain unvoiced.

Target Buyers: CFOs, Investment Committees, CIOs presenting business
cases, Corporate Strategy teams.

Decision Domains: Cloud consolidation, Data Center exits, SAP/ERP
modernization, Security platform investments, AI platforms & data
foundations, major vendor renewals.

Deliverables:
  Decision Dossier (6–8 pages): Single executive narrative with one
  board recommendation backed by evidence.
  Risk Register (Board Version): Top unvarnished risks, mitigations,
  and ownership.
  Architecture & Operating Model Implications: Second-order impacts
  on governance, operations, and security.
  Commercial Sanity Check: Uncovered missing costs, unsafe assumptions,
  and required vendor conditions.

Execution Timeline: Days 1–3 (Review & Discovery) → Days 4–7
(Triangulate & Challenge) → Days 8–10 (Deliver Dossier & Executive
Briefing).

Financial Benchmarks: Up to 30% reduction in cloud run-rate within
6 months; 20% reduction in TCO.


2. Cloud & AI Program Exposure — Board Reality Check
Format & Timebox: 3 weeks | 3 execution phases | 1 independent
audit-grade view.

Problem Solved: Escalating cloud spend and uncoordinated AI pilots
where the board lacks clarity on whether spend generates real value
or funds vendor momentum.

Target Buyers: Boards of Directors, Audit & Risk Committees, CIOs, CFOs.

Deliverables:
  Board Brief (10–12 slides): Plain-language executive presentation
  for non-technical board members.
  Exposure Map: Top 10 failure modes across cost, security, delivery,
  compliance, and operating model.
  Decision Tree: Structured Continue / Stop / Rescope framework with
  enforceable prerequisites.
  90-Day Stabilization Plan: Actionable roadmap to restore governance
  and reduce audit exposure.

Execution Timeline: Week 1 (Stakeholder Interviews & Doc Review) →
Week 2 (Triangulation & Gap Analysis) → Week 3 (Board-Ready
Synthesis & Q&A).


3. Vendor Power Reset — Contract-to-Delivery Forensics
Format & Timebox: 2–4 weeks | Forensic-grade evidence gathering.

Problem Solved: Vendor owns the delivery narrative; constant change
requests and cost escalation without clear, delivered outcomes.

Target Buyers: CIOs, Heads of Infrastructure/Cloud, Procurement Leaders.

Deliverables:
  Contract-to-Delivery Traceability Map: Line-by-line trace contrasting
  contract promises, actual delivery, and invoicing.
  Ambiguity Kill List: Specific contract clauses and process gaps
  causing financial leakage.
  Governance Reset Pack: Re-established RACI, decision rights, change
  control, and acceptance criteria.
  Negotiation Brief: Strategic playbook outlining specific customer
  leverage points prior to renewal.

Financial Impact Targets: 30% Commercial Recovery; 80% Change Request
Suppression; 60% Delivery Risk Mitigation; 90% Timeline Alignment.


4. Executive & Technical Coaching
Format: Ongoing | Embedded directly in active programs | Outcome-funded.

Coaching Tracks: Enterprise Sales Coaching, Business Development
Coaching, Leader Uplift in Real Programs.

Engagement Models: Outcome-Funded Advisory, Co-Invested Initiatives,
Executive-Level Stewardship.

90-Day Onboarding Structure: Weeks 1–2 (Alignment Workshop) →
Weeks 2–8 (In-Flight Coaching) → Weeks 4–10 (Governance Uplift) →
Weeks 10–12 (Value Review & Next Wave).

Business Development as a Service (BDaaS) Fee Structure

All commissions accrue upon contract signature/milestones and are
disbursed exclusively in proportion to collected customer revenue:

  Introduction-Only: 5–10% GM (~1–3% Rev)
    Introduced scope only; excludes expansions/renewals.

  Co-Sell: 10–15% GM (~3–5% Rev)
    Initial deal + expansions within 12 months.

  Full-Cycle BD: 15–25% GM (~5–8% Rev)
    All Year 1 collected revenue (incl. T&M and change requests).

  Strategic Deals: Tiered 20–30% GM (~6–10% Rev)
    Year 1 full; renewals at declining tiered rates.

  Account Farming: 5–15% Rev (tiered)
    Year 1 full; declining rates in Years 2–3.

  Retainer: Monthly Retainer + 5–10% GM
    All Year 1 revenue from deals closed during retainer window.
